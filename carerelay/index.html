What I worked on

I'm an AI software developer on CareRelay at MYB Inc., building it with the product team for WNY Medical. The goal: take routine call triage off the front desk without ever letting an urgent call wait.

  • Voice pipelineVapi and Twilio WebSockets paired with a GLM-4 model deployed through Bedrock Custom Model Import.
  • EHR integrationCall audio to structured HL7/FHIR, delivered by encrypted webhook into MedEnt queues.
  • InfrastructureThree AWS environments in Terraform, GitHub Actions CI/CD, Sentry and CloudWatch monitoring.
  • SafetyBedrock Guardrails, KMS encryption, and automatic emergency escalation with staff alerted to review each one.

How a call moves through it

Four steps, from the first ring to the patient chart.

  1. Answer

    The call is picked up by the voice agent and streamed to the model over Twilio Media Streams.

  2. Classify

    The model talks with the patient, works out the reason for the call, and checks for urgency.

  3. Escalate

    Urgent calls trigger automatic emergency escalation, including ambulance dispatch when needed. Staff are alerted to review each one.

  4. Chart

    The call becomes an HL7/FHIR message delivered by encrypted webhook into the EHR queue.

HIPAA safeguards

Bedrock Guardrails on every model response, KMS encryption across Terraform-managed AWS accounts, encrypted webhooks into the EHR, and least-privilege IAM. Stack: AWS Bedrock, Vapi, Twilio Media Streams, HL7/FHIR, KMS, Terraform.
