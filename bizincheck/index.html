What I owned

I designed and built BizInCheck at MYB Inc. and run it in production. The goal was simple: stop couriering cheques between offices for signatures, and stop buying pre-printed stock for every bank account.

  • Desktop clientElectron app for batch preparation, MICR rendering, and printing.
  • Mobile approval portalAngular web portal for cryptographic remote signing.
  • Backend and dataNode.js services, and a migration from SQLite to PostgreSQL with row-level security.
  • ControlsAppend-only audit logs, sequential cheque validation, duplicate payment detection.

How a cheque moves through it

Four steps, from batch to printed cheque.

  1. Prepare

    Accounting builds a cheque batch for any of the 5 companies in the Electron desktop client.

  2. Approve

    The authorized signer reviews and approves it from a phone through the mobile web portal.

  3. Record

    The approval is written to an append-only cryptographic audit log, with sequential numbering and duplicate checks.

  4. Print

    The client renders the bank's exact layout and MICR line, and prints it on blank stock.
