Three production platforms I built and run at MYB Inc. for WNY Medical, a multi-location primary care group, and its partner companies.

CareRelay

AI voice triage for patient calls

Read the case study
Problem
Front desks across 14 clinics triaged every patient call by hand, and call details had to be re-entered for nurses.
What I built
A voice pipeline on Vapi and Twilio Media Streams with a GLM-4 model deployed on AWS Bedrock through Custom Model Import. Each call becomes an HL7/FHIR message, sent by encrypted webhook straight into MedEnt EHR queues. Bedrock Guardrails, KMS encryption across Terraform-managed accounts, and automatic emergency escalation that includes ambulance dispatch.
Result
10,000 to 15,000 calls a month. Front-desk triage workload down 75%. Nurse charting latency down 90%. No data violations to date.

Stack: Bedrock, Vapi, Twilio Media Streams, HL7/FHIR, KMS, Terraform

SoundEasy

Clinical speech-to-text

Problem
15 to 30 providers relied on Dragon NaturallySpeaking licenses for dictation, a recurring cost with no direct path into MedEnt.
What I built
A Python desktop client under 100MB of RAM that streams audio at 500ms to 1s latency and types into active MedEnt fields through Win32 virtual keystrokes. An LLM pass cleans up medical grammar and dosages. No audio is retained, which keeps it HIPAA-safe.
Result
Providers save 30 to 45 minutes of charting a day. An estimated $18,000 to $25,000 a year saved in licensing.

Stack: Python, PyQt, Win32, LLM post-processing, MedEnt EHR

BizInCheck

Multi-company payments and remote cheque signing

Read the case study
Problem
Cheques for 5 companies waited 3 to 5 days for couriered signatures and depended on pre-printed stock.
What I built
An Electron printing client paired with a mobile web portal for cryptographic remote approval. A MICR E-13B canvas rendering engine that prints on blank stock. PostgreSQL row-level security per company, append-only cryptographic audit logs, sequential cheque validation, and duplicate payment detection.
Result
Signing in under 5 minutes. About $24,000 a year saved on couriers. 20+ bank accounts off pre-printed stock. 10,000+ transactions a month and more than $5M in volume.

Stack: Electron, Angular 17, Node.js, PostgreSQL with RLS, Docker
